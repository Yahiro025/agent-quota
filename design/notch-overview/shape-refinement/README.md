# Dropdown silhouette refinement

The user's keyboard-dropdown screenshot supplies the exterior shape only. Its imagery, colors, typography, list contents and internal layout are not design references for the quota app.

The pinned contour is a dropdown connected across its full top width to a wider upper bar, with small reverse-rounded concave shoulders at the side joins, straight body sides, and softly rounded lower corners. The former narrow central raised neck is replaced by this attachment. Orbit's established graphite identity and the three content strategies remain the basis.
